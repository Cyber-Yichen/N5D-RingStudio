# 应用图标

1.1 图标使用内置生图模型生成。最终作品位于 `assets/app-logo.png`，Android 图标资源位于 `res/drawable-nodpi/app_icon.png`；关于页使用同一作品。图标不使用支付宝品牌符号，光环预览中的硬件 Logo 仍按设备实体显示。

## 最终生成提示词

```text
Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: a finished Android launcher app icon, 1024x1024 square, for an open source lighting controller called N5D Ring Studio. Create one elegant, distinctive icon, no text or letters. A continuous luminous multicolor circular light ring, cyan through cobalt blue, violet, coral pink and warm amber, on a deep midnight navy square background #16243B. The ring is clean, thick enough to read at 48 pixels, with a soft controlled glow, a small subtle offset inner arc suggesting motion and a calm dark open center. Precisely centered with 20 percent safe padding all around for Android circle/squircle masks. Modern refined instrument aesthetic, subtle depth, rich colors, crisp silhouette. Full bleed square navy background, no outer rounded square, no phone or mockup, no extraneous decoration, no Alipay symbol, no watermarks. This is the actual deliverable icon artwork, not a presentation sheet.
```
